Anukriti Singh

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Maryland, College Park, working with Prof. Pratap Tokekar at the RAAS Lab. I specialize in perception-driven robot learning and generalization, with a focus on advancing robust manipulation and navigation through foundation-scale vision models, multimodal supervision, and data-efficient policy and reward learning.

My work bridges video, language, and demonstration to real-world skills—leveraging vision-language models, trajectory sketches, and preference-based feedback to enable scalable, generalist robot intelligence.

Before UMD, I completed my Master of Engineering in Robotics at UMD, where I worked on mobile robot navigation and reinforcement learning. I have also previously interned at Nokia Bell Labs and PicsArt AI Research.

Please feel free to reach out!
